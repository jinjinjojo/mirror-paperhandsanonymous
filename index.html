    We admitted we were powerless over the sell button, and that our PnL had become unmanageable.

  7. 07

    Humbly asked the group chat to stop typing “it's so over” at every red candle.

  8. 08

    Made a list of everyone we told “I'll buy back lower,” and became willing to explain why we never did.

  9. 09

    Made amends to our future selves by not market-selling the first red candle.

  10. 10

    Continued to take personal inventory, and when we called the top, promptly admitted it wasn't one.

  11. 11

    Sought, through deep breaths and closed tabs, to improve our conscious contact with the hold button. still working on this one

  12. 12

    Having held through one whole dip, we tried to carry this message to other paper hands, and to practice holding in all our affairs.
